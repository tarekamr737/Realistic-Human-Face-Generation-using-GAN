About the model

A transparent comparison, not a leaderboard

FFHQ preprocessing

Aligned FFHQ images are center-cropped, resized to 64 × 64 pixels, and normalized to the generator output range of −1 to 1.

Generator

A 128-dimensional random latent vector passes through transposed-convolution blocks and produces an RGB synthetic face. The model is always loaded in evaluation mode here.

Reference model

R3GAN FFHQ-256 is an official pretrained generator loaded only when its local source and checkpoint are installed. The app uses its inference generator and never exposes R3GAN training or fine-tuning.

Discriminator

The discriminator is used only while training in Colab. It never runs in this local app and is not needed for generation.

Known limits

Our model is a 64px educational DCGAN; R3GAN is a pretrained 256px reference. A shared numeric seed is reproducible, but the models use different latent spaces and resolutions, so outputs are not image-paired or a fair equal-resolution benchmark.
